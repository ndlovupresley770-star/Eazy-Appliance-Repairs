Common Dishwasher Faults We Fix

Identify your dishwasher issue below. Our mobile vans across Gauteng carry original replacement pumps, heating elements, valves, and door seals for on-site repairs.

Dishwasher Not Draining

Water remains pooled in the bottom tub after the cycle finishes. Caused by a clogged drain pump impeller, obstructed drain hose, or failed check valve.

  • Drain Pump Replacements
  • Hose & Non-Return Valve Fixes
Book Diagnostic Visit

Dishes Still Dirty / Cold Water

Cycle runs, but food residue remains or water fails to heat up. Caused by a burnt-out inline flow heater, clogged spray arms, or failed diverter motor.

  • Inline Heater & NTC Swaps
  • Spray Arm & Wash Pump Service
Book Diagnostic Visit

Leaking Water onto Floor

Water pools beneath or in front of the unit during operation. Caused by a degraded door gasket seal, worn sump gasket, or cracked water inlet valve.

  • Perimeter Door Gasket Swaps
  • Solenoid Inlet Valve Replacement
Book Diagnostic Visit

Loud Humming or Grinding

Harsh buzzing or rattling noise during wash or drain cycles. Points to foreign debris stuck in the pump impeller or worn circulation motor bearings.

  • Circulation Pump Overhauls
  • Impeller Debris Extraction
Book Diagnostic Visit

Soap Door Not Opening

Detergent tablet remains undissolved in the dispenser door post-wash. Usually caused by a wax motor actuator failure or broken door latch mechanism.

  • Dispenser Module Replacement
  • Door Latch & Interlock Fixes
Book Diagnostic Visit

Error Code Displayed / Beeping

Machine halts mid-cycle and flashes fault codes (e.g., E15, E24, i20). Caused by triggered flood safety switches, sensor faults, or PCB control errors.

  • AquaStop Float Switch Reset
  • Control Module Diagnostics
Book Diagnostic Visit

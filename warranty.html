What's Covered

Warranty Cover at a Glance

A clear summary of what we will fix at no charge, and the situations the warranty does not cover.

What is covered

  • Replacement parts supplied and fitted by Eazy Appliance Repairs.
  • Labor to return and correct the same fault we repaired.
  • Replacement of a defective part within the 6-month warranty period.
  • Re-inspection and testing of the repaired component.

What is not covered

  • New or unrelated faults in other parts of the appliance.
  • Damage from misuse, accidents, spills, pests, flooding, lightning or power surges.
  • Repairs or tampering by anyone other than Eazy Appliance Repairs after our repair.
  • Faults caused by household plumbing, drainage or electrical supply.
  • Consumables and cosmetic items, such as filters, bulbs and trims, unless we replaced them.

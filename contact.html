Before You Call

Help Us Get It Right the First Time

A few details let our dispatch desk send the right technician with the right parts, so your repair is more likely to be finished in a single visit.

  • The appliance type, brand and model number (usually on a sticker inside the door or on the back)
  • What the appliance is doing, and any error code on the display
  • Your suburb, street address and any gate, estate or building access details
  • Your preferred time, and the best number to reach you on

Repairs are carried out on-site at your home or business. Our technicians come to you, so there is nothing to carry or drop off.

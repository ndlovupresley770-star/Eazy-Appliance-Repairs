Helpful Tips for Midrand Customers

Estate access

Most Midrand estates keep an access list. Add our technician's name to your visitor list, or give us your gate contact when booking, so we can get to you without delay.

Not every fault is a broken part. In newer homes we regularly find unlevelled washers, incorrect drain heights or crushed dryer vents. Our technicians check the installation as part of every diagnosis.

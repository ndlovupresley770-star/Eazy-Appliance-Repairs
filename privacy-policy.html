1. Who we are

Eazy Appliance Repairs ("we", "us", "our") provides on-site repairs for household and commercial appliances in Sandton, Randburg, Midrand, Rosebank and surrounding parts of Gauteng. This policy explains how we handle your personal information when you visit our website, contact us or book a repair.

We are the "responsible party" for the personal information described in this policy under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

2. What information we collect

We only collect information that we need to respond to you and carry out your repair. Depending on how you deal with us, this may include:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Service details: your suburb and service address, the appliance type, brand, model, the fault you describe and access instructions such as gate or building details.
  • Job records: quotes, invoices, parts fitted, repair dates and warranty information.
  • Messages: the content of calls, WhatsApp messages and emails you send us.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser shares automatically when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view.

3. How and why we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • respond to enquiries and callback requests, and schedule and carry out repairs;
  • provide quotes, invoices and warranty service, and keep records of work done;
  • contact you about your booking, such as arrival times or follow-up;
  • improve our website and services; and
  • comply with our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We process your information because it is necessary to perform a contract with you or take steps at your request before a contract, because we have a legitimate interest in running and improving our business, because the law requires it, or because you have given your consent. We do not use your information for unrelated purposes.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary with:

  • our technicians and office staff, so they can attend your repair;
  • suppliers, for example when ordering parts for your appliance;
  • service providers that help us run our business, such as communication, payment, accounting and website tools; and
  • authorities, courts or advisers where we are required or permitted by law.

Where we use service providers, we expect them to handle your information securely and only for the purpose we engaged them for.

5. Our website, cookies and third parties

Our pages load fonts, icons and images from third-party providers (for example Google Fonts, Font Awesome and image hosting services). When your browser requests these, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

When you message us on WhatsApp, the message is also handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. Links to other websites are provided for convenience, and we are not responsible for how those sites handle information.

If we add analytics or advertising cookies in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your information only for as long as we need it for the purposes described above. Job records and warranty information are kept for at least the warranty period, and certain invoice and accounting records are kept for the periods required by South African law. When we no longer need information, we delete or de-identify it.

7. How we protect your information

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including limiting access to those who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a security compromise affects your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by POPIA.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you, and request access to it;
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive or unlawfully obtained;
  • object to the processing of your information, or withdraw consent you have given us, at any time; and
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) if you believe we have not handled your information properly. Contact details are available at inforegulator.org.za.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before we act on a request.

9. Children's information

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. We encourage you to review it periodically.

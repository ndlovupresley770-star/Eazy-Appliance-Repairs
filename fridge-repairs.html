Common Fridge & Freezer Faults We Fix

Identify your appliance issue below. Our mobile vans across Gauteng are stocked with genuine spare parts to resolve these problems on-site.

Fridge Not Cooling / Warm

Refrigeration compartment failing to reach cold temperatures while the light stays on. Usually caused by thermostat failure, faulty sensors, or gas leaks.

  • On-Site Gas Regassing
  • Thermostat & Sensor Swaps
Book Diagnostic Visit

Excessive Ice & Frost Build-Up

Heavy frost clogging freezer drawers or thick ice layers forming on the back wall. Typically points to a faulty defrost timer, heater element, or worn door seal.

  • Defrost Heater Replacement
  • Door Gasket & Seal Swaps
Book Diagnostic Visit

Compressor Clicking or Buzzing

Repetitive clicking noises every few minutes or a heavy humming sound coming from the back. Indicates a failing start relay, overload protector, or motor fault.

  • Start Relay & Capacitor Fixes
  • Sealed Motor Overhauls
Book Diagnostic Visit

Water Leaking On Floor

Puddles forming under vegetable crispers or pooling beneath the unit. Usually caused by blocked defrost drain channels, cracked drain pans, or faulty water valves.

  • Drain Line Unblocking
  • Inlet Valve & Tray Repairs
Book Diagnostic Visit

Refrigerant Gas Leaks

Compressor runs continuously without cooling, accompanied by a chemical odor or hissed release. Requires nitrogen pressure testing, leak sealing, and gas recharge.

  • Nitrogen Pressure Leak Test
  • R134a & R600a Gas Refill
Book Diagnostic Visit

Loud Fan or Motor Noises

Squealing, grinding, or rattling sounds that stop when the fridge door is opened. Caused by worn fan motor bearings or fan blades hitting ice accumulations.

  • Evaporator Fan Swaps
  • Condenser Fan Servicing
Book Diagnostic Visit

Frequently Asked Fridge & Freezer Repair Questions

Everything you need to know about our on-site refrigeration repair services across Sandton, Randburg, Midrand, Rosebank, and wider Gauteng.

Continuous running or a warm interior is usually caused by dirty condenser coils, a faulty thermostat, a failing defrost timer, or a refrigerant gas leak. Our mobile technicians carry diagnostic gauges and replacement components to resolve cooling issues on-site.

A repetitive clicking sound typically points to a failing compressor start relay or an overheating overload protector trying to kick-start the motor. We carry OEM replacement relays to get your compressor running safely again.

Excess frost or frozen evaporator coils usually stem from a burnt-out defrost heater element, a blown thermal fuse, or a torn door gasket letting in warm, humid outside air. We test defrost systems and replace seals on the spot.

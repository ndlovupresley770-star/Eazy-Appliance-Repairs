Who We Are

Repairs That Come to You, Done Properly

Eazy Appliance Repairs exists for one simple reason: when an appliance breaks, you shouldn't have to carry it anywhere, wait weeks for an answer or guess what the bill will be.

Our certified technicians travel in fully stocked mobile vans across Sandton, Randburg, Midrand and Rosebank. They diagnose the fault in your home or business, give you a fixed quote before any work begins and, in most cases, finish the repair in a single visit.

Every repair uses genuine factory-original parts where possible and is backed by a written 6-month parts and labor warranty.

What We Stand For

The Principles Behind Every Repair

Simple promises that shape how our technicians work in your home.

Honest, Upfront Pricing

You receive a fixed quote after diagnosis and before any work starts. If you go ahead with the repair, the diagnostic fee is waived.

Genuine Parts

We fit factory-original parts wherever possible, and our vans carry the common pumps, belts, elements and seals for major brands.

Backed in Writing

Every repair carries a written 6-month parts and labor warranty, so you can rely on the work long after we leave.

Certified Technicians

Our technicians are trained to diagnose before they replace, finding the real cause of a fault rather than swapping parts and hoping.

Straight Advice

If a repair isn't worth it, we'll tell you. Our job is to help you make the right decision, not to sell you the biggest fix.

Speed When It Matters

A warm fridge or flooded laundry can't wait. Book before 12:00 PM for priority call-outs and the best chance of a same-day repair.

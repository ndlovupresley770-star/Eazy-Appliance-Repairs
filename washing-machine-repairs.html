Common Washing Machine Faults We Fix

Identify your laundry appliance issue below. Our mobile vans across Gauteng carry genuine replacement parts to resolve these problems on-site.

Washer Not Draining Water

Water pooling inside the drum at the end of a cycle. Usually caused by a clogged drain pump filter, blocked hose, or failed drainage motor.

  • Drain Pump Unblocking & Repair
  • Filter & Hose Clearing
Book Diagnostic Visit

Drum Not Spinning / Agitating

Motor runs but the drum remains stationary during wash or spin cycles. Typically points to a snapped drive belt, worn motor carbon brushes, or lid switch failure.

  • Drive Belt Replacement
  • Carbon Brush Swaps
Book Diagnostic Visit

Water Leaking Under Unit

Puddles forming around the base of the machine. Usually caused by a torn front door boot gasket, cracked inlet valve, or loose hose connection.

  • Door Seal / Gasket Swaps
  • Inlet Hose & Valve Fixing
Book Diagnostic Visit

Loud Noise & Excessive Shaking

Banging or roaring sounds during high-speed spin cycles, or the machine walking across the floor. Caused by worn drum bearings or fatigued shock absorbers.

  • Drum Bearing Overhauls
  • Shock Absorber Replacement
Book Diagnostic Visit

Cycle Stops or Error Code

Washer halts unexpectedly mid-program or flashes electronic error lights. Often indicates a faulty door lock mechanism, water heating element, or control board glitch.

  • Door Interlock Replacement
  • Electronic PCB Diagnostics
Book Diagnostic Visit

No Power / Won't Turn On

Control panel lights fail to illuminate when powered on. Usually stems from a blown thermal fuse, tripped internal power switch, or damaged line cord.

  • Power Switch & Fuse Repairs
  • Wiring Harness Testing

Frequently Asked Washing Machine Repair Questions

Everything you need to know about our on-site laundry appliance repair services across Sandton, Randburg, Midrand, Rosebank, and wider Gauteng.

Failure to drain is usually caused by a clogged drain pump filter, a blocked discharge hose, or a failed drain pump motor. Our mobile technicians carry replacement pumps and extraction tools to clear drain blockages on-site.

A drum that refuses to spin often indicates a snapped drive belt, worn motor carbon brushes, a faulty door interlock switch, or an unbalanced load sensor error. We test drive assemblies and replace worn belts or brushes in a single visit.

Excessive noise and vibration are typically caused by worn drum bearings, damaged shock absorbers, loose counterweights, or worn suspension springs. We overhaul drum bearings and replace suspension struts on-site.

Front-door leaks are usually caused by a torn or moldy rubber door boot seal (bellows). Leaks underneath often stem from cracked tub-to-pump hoses, loose inlet valves, or worn main shaft seals.

All replacement parts installed—including drive belts, drain pumps, door seals, shock absorbers, and valves—are backed by our written 6-Month Guarantee covering both parts and labor.

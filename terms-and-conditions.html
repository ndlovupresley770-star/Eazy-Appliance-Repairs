2. Our services

We diagnose and repair household and commercial appliances, including fridges and freezers, washing machines, tumble dryers and dishwashers, at your premises in our service areas. Estimated arrival times and typical repair times on our website are guides only and are not guaranteed, because they depend on traffic, call volume and the nature of the fault.

3. Bookings and access

You can book by phone, WhatsApp or through our website. Callback requests and online forms are requests for a booking and are not confirmed until we confirm a time with you.

Please make sure someone aged 18 or over is present, and that our technician can safely reach the appliance. If your property is in a complex or estate, please arrange gate or building access in advance.

4. Diagnosis, quotes and pricing

Our technician will inspect the appliance and give you a fixed quote for the repair before any work begins. Work only starts once you approve the quote. Prices shown on our website and in our guides are typical ranges and are not quotes.

If you approve the repair, the initial diagnostic inspection fee is waived. If you decline the repair, the diagnostic inspection fee will apply, and we will tell you the amount before the inspection begins.

5. Payment

Payment is due on completion of the work, unless we have agreed otherwise in writing. We will tell you which payment methods we accept when you book. You will receive an invoice showing the work done and the parts fitted.

6. Cancellations and rescheduling

Please tell us as early as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule. If a technician arrives and cannot gain access, or the appointment is cancelled on arrival, a diagnostic or call-out fee may be charged.

7. Parts

We aim to fit genuine factory-original (OEM) parts. Where a part is unavailable or no longer manufactured, we will discuss suitable alternatives with you before fitting them. Parts that have been replaced remain our property to dispose of, unless you ask us to leave them with you.

8. Warranty

Repairs are covered by our written 6-month parts and labor warranty, subject to the conditions on our warranty page and your invoice.
